# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want TaskFlow to be a friendly and respectful place to learn and work
together, for everyone, whatever their background or experience level.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and kind to your teammates.
- Give feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Accept feedback gracefully and respond to reviews in a reasonable time.
- Ask questions when something is unclear. Nobody knows everything.
- Credit other people's work.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, harassment or discrimination of any kind
- Personal attacks or mocking someone's code or questions
- Publishing someone else's private information
- Any other behaviour that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies everywhere we work on the project: issues, pull requests,
code reviews, commit messages and the team chat.

## Reporting a problem

If you experience or notice unacceptable behaviour, tell a teammate you
trust or the course instructor. Reports are handled respectfully and
with discretion.

## Enforcement

The team and the course instructor may remove or edit comments and other
contributions that do not follow this code of conduct.
